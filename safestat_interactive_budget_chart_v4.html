What each bar represents

Each bar shows the total Ballmer Group investment for one city cohort across its full five-year funded lifecycle — the complete commitment from launch through sunset.

Year 1 (8 cities): Covers this cohort's full five-year lifecycle.

Year 2 (17 cities): Covers this cohort's full five-year lifecycle — the largest cohort and the peak of the platform buildout.

Year 3 (13 cities): Covers this cohort's full five-year lifecycle at significantly lower per-city cost as the CoE reaches maturity.

Year 4 (12 cities): Covers this cohort's full five-year lifecycle at steady-state platform rates — the lowest per-city cost of any cohort.

Year 5: No new request. All cohorts are either fully funded or self-sustaining. This gap is intentional — it marks Ballmer Group's clean exit from the funding commitment.

Reading the cost curves

Each line shows the per-city platform rate at the start of that budget year — the actual unit cost a city sees when it enters or continues on SafeStat.

Solid blue line: What it costs to onboard a new city that year (stand-up + license). Declines as CoE matures and group delivery scales.

Dashed blue line: What an existing city pays in its first full support year (ongoing support + license). The recurring rate it carries at steady state.

By Year 4 the two lines nearly converge — the cost of onboarding a new city approaches the cost of maintaining an existing one. That's the efficiency floor.

Reading the blended rates

The blended rate lines show the average annual cost per city across the full lifecycle — Ballmer's share (steel blue dotted) and the all-in gross cost (orange solid). Field-Informed Enhancements are excluded from both calculations as a platform-level cost not attributable per city.

Use the lifecycle slider to see how extending the platform horizon beyond the five-year funded window changes the per-city economics.

As cities self-fund post-sunset years, the gross rate continues to decline while Ballmer's rate holds steady — illustrating the growing efficiency return on the original investment.
